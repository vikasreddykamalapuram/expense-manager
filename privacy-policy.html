MoneyIQ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03 · Applies to the MoneyIQ app on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web.

Summary

  • MoneyIQ is a local-first personal-finance manager. Everything you enter is stored on your device, and the app works fully offline.
  • You do not need an account. If you never sign in, nothing you enter leaves your device.
  • If you choose to sign in and turn on sync, your financial data is encrypted on your device, with a passphrase only you know, before it is uploaded. We cannot read it.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and your data is never sold or shared.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored in the app's private storage (an IndexedDB database) on your device: transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, portfolio, bill reminders, receipt images and settings. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes it permanently.

Optional sign-in and cross-device sync

You can sign in with Apple or Google to keep your devices in sync. Sync uses a database hosted for us by Supabase. When you sign in, we receive the following from the sign-in provider:

  • Email address. With Sign in with Apple you can choose "Hide My Email", and we then receive only a private relay address.
  • Name, if the provider shares it. It is shown in the app.
  • A user ID issued by the provider and by our database. It identifies your account.

We never receive your Apple or Google password.

When sync is turned on, the following is uploaded:

  • Your financial data, end-to-end encrypted. Before anything leaves your device, the content of every record is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is protected by your sync passphrase and by a recovery code shown to you once. Neither the passphrase nor the key is ever sent to us, so we cannot read your amounts, notes, categories, accounts or holdings. If you lose both your passphrase and your recovery code, we cannot recover your cloud data.
  • What is not encrypted (metadata). To sync, the server needs to know some facts about each record. These are not encrypted:
    • how many records you have, and which profile each belongs to;
    • when each record was created, changed or deleted;
    • a random ID that the app generates for each installation, a device label such as "iOS 10/3/2026", and when each device last synced.
    None of these reveal what a record contains.

How it is protected: TLS in transit; encryption at rest by our hosting provider; and row-level security, so the database only returns rows that belong to the signed-in account.

You can stop syncing at any time by signing out: Settings → Cloud & Sync → Sign out.

Features that run only on your device

  • Voice entry (microphone and speech recognition): your speech is turned into text on the device. The audio and the transcript are never uploaded or stored.
  • Receipt scanning (camera and photos): the image is read on the device. Images are never uploaded.
  • App lock (Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint): handled by your device's operating system. MoneyIQ never receives biometric data.
  • Assistant: questions about your spending are answered on the device, from your own data.
  • Reminders: bill and daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device. No server is involved.

Auto-detect transactions (optional, off by default)

Every source below is opt-in and runs entirely on your device. Each produces suggestions in an in-app review queue. Nothing is saved as a transaction, transmitted or shared until you confirm it.

  • Shared messages (all platforms): when you share a bank SMS or payment message into MoneyIQ, we read it on the device to pre-fill a transaction. On iPhone this works through a Shortcut you set up yourself.
  • Bank notifications (Android only, opt-in): if you turn this on and grant Android "notification access", the app reads transaction-style notifications on the device to suggest transactions. Other notifications are ignored, only a short in-memory buffer is kept, and notification content is never stored or uploaded.
  • Gmail, read-only (Android and web only, opt-in): if you tap "Scan Gmail" and grant read-only access, the app reads previews of recent bank and payment emails on the device to suggest transactions. MoneyIQ's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only on the device for this feature, is never transferred to others, and is never used for advertising.

You can turn any of these off at any time in Settings → Notifications → Auto-detect.

Optional legacy Google Drive backup

An older, optional feature lets Google users keep a copy of their data in the hidden app-data folder of their own Google Drive. The backup is a plain, unencrypted copy of your data. Older Drive sync data is encrypted, but its key is stored in the same folder. Either way it is protected by your Google account rather than by a passphrase. It goes directly from your device to your Drive: it is never sent to us, and we have no access to your Drive. It lives under Settings → Cloud & Sync → Advanced, where you can also delete it. For encrypted sync, use the sign-in sync described above instead.

Stock prices

To show reference prices for stocks you record, the app downloads a public price file that we publish. Nothing about you or your holdings is sent.

Crash reporting

The app contains crash-reporting code (Sentry), but it is turned off in every published version, so no crash data is sent. If we ever turn it on, we will update this policy first.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No analytics on your usage or your financial content.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data.
  • We do not connect to your bank, read your SMS inbox, or ask for bank credentials.

Permissions

  • Internet: to sign in, sync (if you turn it on) and fetch stock prices.
  • Camera and photos: to scan a receipt. Processed on the device.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: for voice entry. Processed on the device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint: for the optional app lock.
  • Notifications (and, on Android, exact alarms): for reminders you set.
  • Notification access (Android, optional): only for "Bank notifications" under Auto-detect. You can revoke it in system settings.
  • Gmail read-only (Android and web, optional): only when you tap "Scan Gmail". You can revoke it in your Google Account.

How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • In the cloud: for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and all synced records are deleted immediately. Copies in our hosting provider's routine backups expire within a short period.

Deleting your account and data

  • Delete your account from the app: Settings → Data → Danger Zone → Delete Account, then type DELETE to confirm. On phones, Settings is under More. This permanently deletes your account and every synced record, then erases the data on this device.
  • If you never signed in: the same button erases everything on the device. Uninstalling the app does the same.
  • If you can no longer open the app: email us from the address you signed in with. We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Your rights

You can see and correct everything we hold by using the app. You can export it at any time (Settings → Data → Export) and delete it as described above. For any other request about your personal data, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

MoneyIQ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.
