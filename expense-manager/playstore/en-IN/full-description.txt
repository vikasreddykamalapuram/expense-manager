MoneyIQ is a private, local-first personal-finance manager built for people who want to actually own their financial data.

⚡ WHY MONEYIQ

• 100% local storage — your transactions live on your device by default
• No ads, no subscription, no data mining, ever
• Optional real-time cross-device sync with a private, row-isolated cloud database
• Works fully offline, forever

💰 TRACK EVERYTHING

• Income, expenses, and transfers across bank accounts, credit cards, and loans
• Hierarchical categories with custom icons
• Multi-account support with running balances
• Bill reminders with local push notifications (fires at 9 AM on due date)
• Recurring transactions with auto-scheduling

📊 UNDERSTAND YOUR MONEY

• Six-view analytics: overview, category breakdown, trends, income vs expense, budget comparison, period comparison
• Monthly financial reports
• Financial health score across savings rate, budget adherence, and debt ratio
• Smart auto-categorization that learns from your history
• AI chat assistant with support for English and Hindi
