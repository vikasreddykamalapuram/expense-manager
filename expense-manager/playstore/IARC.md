## Data collection
The questionnaire may ask about personal/sensitive data. MoneyIQ collects account email,
user ID, and financial data **only** for optional cloud sync, encrypted in transit, never shared.
This is declared in detail in the separate **Data safety** form (see `playstore/ASSETS.md`).

## After submitting
Play generates region-specific ratings (ESRB Everyone, PEGI 3, USK 0, etc.). These appear on the
store listing automatically — no manual entry needed.
