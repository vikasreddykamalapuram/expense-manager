MoneyIQ — Account & Data Deletion

App: MoneyIQ · Developer: Vikas Kamalapuram · Package: io.github.vikasreddykamalapuram.moneyiq

This page explains how to request deletion of your MoneyIQ account and the data associated with it. It applies to the optional cloud-sync account you create when you sign in with Google or Microsoft. If you never signed in, MoneyIQ has no account or server-side data for you at all — see "Local data" below.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest, immediate)

  1. Open MoneyIQ and go to Settings → Advanced.
  2. Tap Delete cloud sync data and confirm.

This immediately removes all your rows from the cloud sync database — including your encryption key and every sync delta. Your on-device data is preserved so the app keeps working locally. Signing out afterwards (Settings → Cross-device sync → Sign out) stops any further sync.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, email vikasreddykamalapuram@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "MoneyIQ account deletion request". Your cloud data will be deleted within 30 days and you will receive a confirmation.

What data is deleted

  • Your account identifiers: email address and provider-issued user ID.
  • All synced financial data: transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, recurring rules, stock transactions, and bill reminders.
  • Sync metadata: device names, sync timestamps, and your sync encryption key.

What is retained

  • Nothing is retained after deletion beyond what is required by law. There are no backups kept beyond the deletion window, and your financial content is never used for analytics or advertising.
  • Local data on your device is not affected by a cloud deletion. To remove it, uninstall MoneyIQ or clear the app's storage — this permanently erases the on-device IndexedDB database.

Local data (no sign-in)

MoneyIQ is local-first. If you use it without signing in, everything you enter stays only in your browser/app storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently, and no deletion request is needed because nothing ever left your device.
