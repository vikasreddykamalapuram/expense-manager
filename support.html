Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Tap Continue without account on the first screen. Your data stays on the device. An account is only needed to sync between devices.

Where is Settings on my phone?

Tap More in the bottom bar, then Settings.

How do I sync between devices?

Settings → Cloud & Sync → Sign in to enable sync. Sign in with Apple or Google, then choose a sync passphrase. Use the same account and passphrase on your other devices.

I forgot my sync passphrase

Use the recovery code you were shown when you set up sync. Your data is end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot reset the passphrase or read your data. Without the passphrase or the recovery code, the cloud copy cannot be recovered. The data on your devices is not affected.

How do I back up my data?

Settings → Data → Export → JSON Backup (Full) saves a file you can keep anywhere. Restore JSON Backup on the same screen brings it back.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Data → Danger Zone → Delete Account. See Account & data deletion.

Face ID or fingerprint unlock stopped working

Check that the app still has permission in your device settings. Your PIN always works as a fallback.

Stock prices look out of date

Prices are delayed reference prices, refreshed several times a day during NSE market hours. They are for information only. A few delisted or suspended stocks show no price.
