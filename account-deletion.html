MoneyIQ — Account & Data Deletion

App: MoneyIQ · Developer: Vikas Kamalapuram · App ID: io.github.vikasreddykamalapuram.moneyiq · Last updated: 2026-10-03

This page explains how to delete your MoneyIQ account and all the data associated with it. An account exists only if you signed in with Apple or Google to sync between devices. If you never signed in, all your data is on your device, and the same in-app button erases it.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open MoneyIQ and go to Settings. On phones, Settings is under More in the bottom bar.
  2. Open Data and scroll to Danger Zone.
  3. Tap Delete Account, type DELETE, and confirm.

If you are signed in, this permanently deletes your account and every synced record from our server, then erases the data on this device. If you are not signed in, it erases the data on this device. Either way, the app then returns to its first screen. Deletion cannot be undone, so export your data first (Settings → Data → Export) if you want a copy.

If you use MoneyIQ on more than one device, the data already on your other devices stays there until you delete it on each of them, or uninstall the app.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, email vikasreddykamalapuram@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "MoneyIQ account deletion request". If you used Sign in with Apple with "Hide My Email", send it from the Apple ID email and tell us so. We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name and user IDs.
  • All synced records: transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, recurring rules, stock transactions and bill reminders. These are stored encrypted, and are deleted whether or not you remember your sync passphrase.
  • Sync data: device IDs and labels, sync timestamps, and your encrypted sync key.
  • With in-app deletion, everything stored on that device.

What is kept

  • Nothing, except where the law requires it. Copies in our hosting provider's routine backups expire within a short period.
  • If you used the optional legacy Google Drive backup, that backup is in your own Google Drive. Remove it from Settings → Cloud & Sync → Advanced before deleting your account, or from your Google Drive settings (Manage apps).

Signing out instead

To stop syncing but keep your account, use Settings → Cloud & Sync → Sign out. Your data stays on the device.
